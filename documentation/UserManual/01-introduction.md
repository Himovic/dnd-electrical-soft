# Introduction

**D&D Electrical Engineering Suite** is an integrated environment for electrical design: schematics, symbol libraries, wiring, cables and terminals, PLC I/O, control-cabinet layout, engineering calculations, validation and documentation — in one project.

Author & Developer: **D&D | Data&Development** · © 2026 D&D | Data&Development. All rights reserved.

## What makes it different

- **Real connectivity.** Connections are stored as explicit references between pins and junctions; potentials are computed from them, never guessed from drawing proximity.
- **One source of truth.** Device tags, wire numbers, cross-references, terminal diagrams, BOM and reports are all derived from the same project data.
- **Transparent calculations.** Every calculation shows its inputs, formulas, steps, assumptions and a validation status.
- **Safe by default.** Atomic saves, rolling backups, autosave recovery and project locking protect your work.

## Where to start

1. Open a demonstration project from the start page (they contain fictional data).
2. Follow the *Your first schematic* tutorial (offered when you open the schematic editor).
3. Read [Interface overview](03-interface-overview).

> Calculation results are preliminary engineering aids. They do not certify compliance with any standard.
